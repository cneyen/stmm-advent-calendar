“Good to know!”

Why an STMM Advent Calendar? Who is it for? What will I discover inside?

Every day in December, to sweeten your day-to-day research, we share a different tip, tool, service, or resource brought to you by the STMM community — anything you found useful and want everyone to know about.

Everyone involved in research at the Medical Faculty Mannheim can contribute content to go behind the doors of the calendar. And everyone can open doors!

Welcome to the calendar!

Something useful awaits.
Choose a day to see behind the door. One new door opens each day.
Doors unlock at midnight in Mannheim (Europe/Berlin time).
Once open, doors remain accessible until the end of December.
